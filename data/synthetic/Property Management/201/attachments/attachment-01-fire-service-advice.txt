Post-incident advice for occupiers

Incident: Kitchen fire (cooking related)
Address: Flat 2, 41 Wexcombe Road, Kingshaven
Date/time: 13/03/2026 22:47
Crew: Red Watch, Kingshaven Central

Action taken:
- Fire extinguished (pan and cooker hob, spread to extractor hood and wall units)
- Electricity to the kitchen circuit isolated at the consumer unit
- Property ventilated

Observations:
- Hallway smoke alarm did not sound. Occupier was alerted by the smell of smoke.
- Smoke damage to kitchen ceiling and hallway.

Advice:
- Do not use the kitchen electrical circuit until it has been inspected by a qualified electrician.
- Inform your landlord and your insurer as soon as possible.
- Replace or repair any smoke alarm that did not operate.
